What it is

What is virtual staging?

Virtual staging is digitally furnishing an empty or dated room in a listing photo with realistic designer furniture and decor — so a buyer scrolling online can instantly picture themselves living there.

It does the job of physical staging (helping buyers feel at home) without the moving trucks, rental fees or weeks of lead time. You get MLS-ready images in a day or two, at a fraction of the cost, and you can restage the same room in a completely different style for a different buyer.

Questions

Virtual staging FAQ

What is virtual staging?

Virtual staging is digitally furnishing an empty or dated room in a listing photo with realistic designer furniture and decor, so buyers can picture themselves living there. It replaces the cost and logistics of physical staging while producing MLS-ready images.

How much does virtual staging cost?

Bella starts at $45 per photo, with volume discounts up to 20%. A typical eight-photo listing runs about $324 to $360. There's no subscription and no minimum, and furniture removal for occupied rooms is available.

How long does it take?

24 to 48 hours from the moment you upload, with unlimited revisions free for two weeks and a rework-or-refund guarantee.

Is virtual staging allowed on the MLS?

Yes. Virtually staged photos are widely used on the MLS as long as they're disclosed as virtually staged. We build every image to MLS specifications and tell you exactly what to note. Confirm the exact wording with your local real estate board.

Can you stage occupied or cluttered rooms?

Yes. We can digitally remove the existing furniture and clutter first, then stage the empty room, so tenant-occupied and lived-in listings still look clean and move-in ready.
