The LA market

Why staging matters more in Los Angeles.

Los Angeles is one of the most competitive, design-literate markets in the country. Buyers here scroll fast, expect a point of view, and can tell a tired listing from a considered one in half a second. An empty room reads as "unfinished"; a beige, generic stage reads as "flip." The listings that win are the ones staged for who actually buys in that pocket of the city.

That's the whole idea behind how we work: real interior designers who dress a Silver Lake bungalow differently than a Bird Streets contemporary, because the buyers are different and the comps are different. In a market where the median list price runs well over seven figures, the photos are doing million-dollar persuasion, and they should look like it.

Los Angeles questions

Virtual staging in LA, answered.

Is virtual staging legal in Los Angeles and California?

Yes. Virtual staging is widely used across LA listings. California requires that a property be represented accurately, so virtually staged photos must be clearly disclosed as virtually staged. We label every image and tell you exactly what to note. Confirm the final wording with CRMLS, TheMLS and your broker.

Which LA neighborhoods do you stage?

All of them: Venice and Santa Monica, the Hollywood Hills and Bird Streets, Silver Lake and Los Feliz, DTLA and the Arts District, Pasadena and San Marino, and family homes across the Valley. We match the interior style to what buyers in that specific pocket expect.

How fast can I get staged photos for an LA listing?

24 to 48 hours from upload, so you can hit the MLS on your timeline. Unlimited revisions are free for two weeks, backed by a rework-or-refund guarantee.

Do you work with the Los Angeles MLS?

Yes. Every image is built to MLS specifications for CRMLS and TheMLS, and we provide the virtual-staging disclosure note LA agents typically add to the listing remarks.
