Context. Improved version of the live Project Brief (delivery.bellavirtualstaging.com/preview-brief). The page below is a clickable front-end mockup. Keep the existing structure, copy voice and brand (single-font Jost, cream/ink/green); these are targeted UX fixes, not a redesign.

  1. Thumbnails + auto-detect in "Which room is which?" Show each uploaded photo's thumbnail beside its room dropdown, and pre-select the room by matching keywords in the filename (map below). Tag it "auto-detected" and clear the tag when the user edits it. Done when: every row shows its image and rooms come pre-filled and editable.
  2. Photo-completeness banner + gating. Show "X of N photos uploaded" (N = ordered count) with a progress bar; the section only completes at N of N. Done when: the banner reflects the real upload count.
  3. State-aware "Send to the studio". Disabled with a reason until all N photos are uploaded and every photo has a room. Reasons cascade: "Upload X more photos" then "Label your rooms" then enabled. Done when: a brief can't be sent with missing photos or unlabeled rooms.
  4. Pick exact looks from the lookbook. In "The look", a "Browse the lookbook" button opens a filterable grid of real sets; the client multi-selects the SKUs they like and they attach to the brief as chips. Wire the grid to the real lookbook data; store selected SKUs on the order. (Optional field — does not gate Send.)
  5. Polish. "✓ Saved" flash on each edit; optional fields labeled "optional"; mobile-responsive; after Send, show confirmation + "photos back by {date}".
