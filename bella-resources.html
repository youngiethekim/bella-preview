Real estate marketing FAQ

What is real estate marketing?

Real estate marketing is how an agent gets a listing in front of the right buyers and gets them to act. It spans the listing's photos and visuals, the listing copy, MLS and portal syndication, social media, email, paid ads, open houses, and the follow-up that turns interest into offers.

What matters most in real estate marketing?

The visuals. Most buyers start online and decide from the first few photos whether to click, so staging, listing photography, floor plans and 3D tours do the heaviest lifting. Strong copy, fast syndication and consistent follow-up then compound that first impression.

How do you market a real estate listing?

Lead with a standout hero photo and staged, MLS-ready visuals, write buyer-focused listing copy, syndicate to the MLS and portals, run a first-72-hours social and email push to your database, host and promote an open house, and follow up with every lead.
